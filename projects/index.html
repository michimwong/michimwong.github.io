Projects

Selected work from my MSC in Social Data Science at the Oxford Internet Institute as well as personal projects

Papers

Foreclosing the Argument: A Computational Approach to Thought-Terminating Clichés in Online Discourse
MSc thesis · University of Oxford · 2026
How humans and LLMs disagree about what counts as a thought-terminating cliché, using 83 trained annotators and 12 LLM configurations on 589 Reddit comments
Detecting Gen-Z Language Register: A Comparative Evaluation of Classifiers
Paper + code · Oxford Internet Institute · 2026
A comparative evaluation of six classifiers—from a word-frequency baseline to GPT-4o-mini—for detecting Gen-Z register in text, tested on both curated and naturalistic Reddit data
The Gail’s Effect: Café Location Expansion and Gentrification in London Neighborhoods
Paper + code · Oxford Internet Institute · 2026
A staggered difference-in-differences study using Callaway-Sant'Anna estimation to test whether Gail's café openings drive gentrification in London neighborhoods—or merely follow it

Other projects

F1's Parenthood Tax
A quasi-experimental study using teammate-normalized qualifying data (2006–2024) to test whether Formula 1 drivers slow down after having their first child—finding a small, statistically insignificant "parenthood tax" on pace
GANNI Consumer Research Survey
A market and consumer research survey exploring brand perception and purchasing behavior for the fashion label GANNI, designed and built in Qualtrics
